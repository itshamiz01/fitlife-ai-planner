# Personalized fitness system: Phase 1 audit and implementation plan

Date: 2026-11-02. Status: inspection and plan only. No new workout system, video demonstrations, database migrations, production changes, or clinical validation are claimed here. This supplements, rather than replaces, `BODY_WORKOUT_ARCHITECTURE_PLAN.md` and `BODY_ASSESSMENT_METHODOLOGY.md`.

## Current application and reuse

- **Architecture:** `full-stack-health-tracker/` is the existing Next.js/React app with PostgreSQL and Drizzle (`src/db/schema.ts`). The separate root application is outside this work.
- **Profile:** `wellnest_users` already holds age, height, current/start/target weight, gender, activity, weight goal, experience, coarse equipment, workout minutes and four fitness focuses. Reuse these; do not duplicate them. The current gender field is not a verified physiological-sex field. The existing nutrition estimator uses gender as an energy-estimation proxy, an important limitation; do not silently use it as biological sex or change its formula as part of this project.
- **Workout today:** `src/lib/planner.ts` generates seven recurring weekdays with two textual activities per day using a limited set of inputs. `wellnest_workouts`, `src/components/views.tsx` and `src/app/api/records/route.ts` support manual edits and a weekday completion boolean. Those booleans are not dated exercise, set or rep histories. Existing suggestions are explicitly described as not individually safety-screened; the app does not have the requested safety-screened exercise library, custom weekly program, execution mode or progression engine.
- **Diet coupling and data preservation:** `src/app/api/profile/route.ts`, `src/app/api/plan/route.ts` and `src/lib/plan-archive.ts` archive and replace **both** meals and workouts when planning details change or a plan is regenerated. New workout generation must be independent and opt-in; must not implicitly regenerate meals, erase manually entered activities, overwrite saved plans, or reinterpret historic checkmarks. Show conflicting workout and nutrition goals without silently changing either.
- **AI:** `src/lib/coach-engine.ts` is a local deterministic coach. `src/lib/coach-provider.ts` optionally uses Groq for intent classification, not validated exercise prescriptions. User messages may be sent to that provider if configured; account records are not sent by that classifier. Do not replace the provider or send new sensitive screening answers to it without explicit authorization. AI explanations cannot override deterministic safety and eligibility checks.
- **Media:** recipe illustrations and a workout-related recipe image exist under `public/images/`; these are not instructional demonstrations. No exercise MP4/WebM/MOV files were found in the public asset tree. No video generation service or video player dependency is present in the inspected app or available toolset. There are currently **zero reviewed exercise demonstration videos**. Do not claim there are generated or licensed videos. Future footage requires verified exercise identity, rights, technical checks and independent qualified review of movement and anatomy; use an explicit unavailable fallback until then. Approval is needed before any paid service.
- **Authentication and isolation:** account/session authentication and origin checks are used in profile, records and plan APIs; record mutations scope IDs to the authenticated user. New tables and endpoints must verify ownership at every parent-child boundary, handle exports and account deletion, and maintain server-only database credentials. Sensitive limitations need data minimization and clear retention/deletion handling. Database policies and role access must be verified for every additive migration.
- **UI:** mobile and desktop use the existing `views.tsx`, profile components and global styles. Preserve the existing features and current approved visual design; incorporate accessible controls and text alternatives rather than relying solely on video or color.
- **Existing draft:** `src/lib/body-assessment.ts`, `scripts/verify-body-assessment.cjs` and `docs/BODY_ASSESSMENT_METHODOLOGY.md` are a standalone, unintegrated arithmetic draft. They are not clinical validation, a live body assessment UI, or exercise clearance. The draft voluntarily withholds adult interpretation under age 20 and for pregnancy/postpartum or uncertain applicability. Its population option is currently broad `asian/other/unspecified`, not the detailed choices requested.

## Safety and evidence boundaries

BMI is a screening metric, not a body-fat measurement, diagnosis or personal ideal weight. The proposed height-based range uses 18.5 and 24.9 times height in metres squared; calculate categories using unrounded values. Asian action points 23 and 27.5 are additional public-health screening points, not new universal diagnostic categories or an ethnicity-specific weight formula. Ask for population reference voluntarily; do not infer it from location. No adult BMI interpretation for minors; retain the current conservative under-20 withholding unless an age-appropriate method is reviewed. For pregnancy/postpartum or uncertain applicability, withhold standard interpretation. Any symptom/health screen must distinguish unknown from no: absent answers do not mean safe. Exercise-related chest pain, fainting or serious symptoms warrant stopping and urgent medical attention, not an automatically substituted workout. Clinician restrictions take precedence.

Research references and scope are recorded in `BODY_WORKOUT_ARCHITECTURE_PLAN.md` and `BODY_ASSESSMENT_METHODOLOGY.md`: WHO 2020 activity guidance, ACSM 2026 resistance-training position stand, CDC BMI guidance, WHO growth references, the 2004 WHO Asian BMI expert consultation, and NICE waist-to-height guidance. Population guidelines do not validate this software or provide a mandatory beginner starting workload. No claims of clinical validation or guaranteed outcomes.

## Proposed incremental implementation

1. **Body assessment:** review and test the existing standalone draft; expand voluntary reference options only as evidence permits, then design an accessible optional screen using existing profile values and explicit applicability. Avoid sex-specific calculations unless actually needed; no unmeasured body-fat estimate or automatic target. Do not modify existing weight history.
2. **Supplemental fitness inputs and safety:** add only missing location, equipment, experience/history, schedule, preferences, and minimal explicit safety answers, with unknown distinct from no. Add account-owned schema only after design review, with reversible additive migration, documented rollback, export/deletion and isolation tests. Do not apply it to production during development.
3. **Exercise library and safety rules:** curate versioned exercise definitions, instructions, equipment and contraindication metadata before generation. Exercise technique and exercise-specific limits require competent professional review. Unknown or affirmative concerning safety answers block general prescriptions rather than pretending they are medical clearance. Do not assume self-selected advanced equals demonstrated ability.
4. **Deterministic generator:** choose from eligible library entries, assign practical warm-up, training and recovery within an explicit time budget, validate all outputs and explain choices. Keep rest days and recovery, fit to actual equipment, screen health constraints, and support goal-specific strategies without promising results. No unchecked AI-generated exercises, videos or numerical prescriptions. Demonstrate distinct synthetic scenarios before any release.
5. **Video pipeline:** define stable asset IDs and manifest fields for source, license, reviewer, status, duration and file paths; build exercise-specific production prompts and player/fallback. Link only a matching, licensed, reviewed clip. Assess metadata and playback automatically; biomechanics and anatomical accuracy require human review. An absent clip must be labeled unavailable rather than falsely presented as a demonstration. 10-second clips remain a release dependency, not something currently available.
6. **Plans and execution:** additive user-owned versioned weekly plans, dated sessions and exercise assignments; explicit set/rep/weight logging and completion, timer, skips and substitutions. Maintain existing recurring workouts separately. Use transactions and idempotency; ensure no generated plan is committed without user action and preserve old plans.
7. **Adaptation, diet and coach:** use recorded performance, discomfort and recovery to offer conservative, explainable, user-approved changes; do not progress automatically through pain or every week. Surface nutrition-goal conflicts without overwriting meal plans. Provider/coach must not invent exercise or media or bypass safety gates.
8. **Release gates:** synthetic profile and age/health/equipment/time/goal boundary tests; migration forward/rollback against an isolated database; multi-account ownership and export/deletion checks; build, lint, type checks and mobile/desktop accessibility and regression checks. Real PostgreSQL concurrency, real-device media tests and independent professional review are separate gates. Document failures and limitations, and seek authorization before any push, production migration or deployment.

## Files likely affected in later stages

Pure assessment and tests: `src/lib/body-assessment.ts`, `scripts/verify-body-assessment.cjs`, methodology docs. Profile and safety: `src/db/schema.ts`, a new additive migration, `src/app/api/profile/`, profile UI and export/account routes. Library, engine and video: new scoped `src/lib/` modules, reviewed asset manifest and exercise media components. Plans, history and UI: new account-scoped workout APIs and components, with integration into `views.tsx`, navigation and existing styles. Exact changes will be reviewed phase by phase; no root app or unapproved provider changes.

## Phase 1 verification and limitations

This phase inspected source, schema, package dependencies, assets and existing documentation. It did not run a new end-to-end test suite, implement new behavior, generate/supply videos, perform professional review, or deploy. Previously tested standalone draft results are documented separately and are not represented as current end-to-end tests.
