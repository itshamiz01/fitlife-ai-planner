# Recipe library implementation

## Scope and compatibility
The existing catalog of 18 recipes remains in place; `src/lib/recipe-expansion.ts` adds 30 recipes (48 unique IDs total) and additional foods. Existing recipe IDs and saved meal snapshots are not rewritten. The library, serving controls and grocery calculation are implemented in the Next.js app only. Migration `0005_grocery_purchases.sql` adds an account-scoped grocery purchase state table; it has not been applied to production. Recipe snapshots are not migrated.

The library supports name search, category, diet, estimated per-serving cost, protein, preparation/cooking-time, and adult energy-comparison filters. The energy comparison contrasts a reference portion with the estimated target for its first listed meal slot; it is not a weight-change prediction or a clinical recommendation. Saved diet and recognized ingredient exclusions are applied before display. Unknown exclusions fail closed; this is not a guarantee against hidden ingredients or manufacturing cross-contact. Check ingredient labels and all seasonings independently. Added recipes provide structured steps, times, storage notes and ingredient quantities. The original 18 instructions are presented as sentence-based steps without changing their text; their cooking times remain unknown, and these steps are not newly validated recipes.

Planner candidates and swaps can use compatible new recipes in their existing meal slots. Variety scoring discourages repeats where affordable. Planner budget calculations use the existing estimated ingredient costs and weekly cap; they cannot guarantee actual market expenditure, availability or that every profile and budget yields a feasible plan. When no feasible plan exists, the existing warning behavior applies. Presets are ₹50, ₹75, ₹100, ₹120, ₹150 and ₹200 per day.

For ingredient-based saved meals, the recipe guide can save 1–4 servings, retaining the initial ingredient quantities and estimates as an embedded baseline for reversible scaling. The server locks the user and meal, checks for stale changes, recognized ingredients, dietary restrictions, priced meals and estimated daily/weekly budget before saving; it resets the enjoyed mark. Library recipes and grocery people controls remain previews. The portions represent multiples of the original saved plan portion, not necessarily appropriate individual dietary portions. Instructions may contain reference quantities requiring manual adjustment. Edits to a manual meal do not retain ingredient-derived snapshots.

Ingredient substitution is limited to carrot/capsicum, spinach/cabbage and banana/papaya at the same raw edible weight; it is not a claim of culinary or nutrient equivalence. The server rechecks the modified ingredient list, estimates and budget, and warns that original instructions need adaptation. Existing snapshots are not changed unless the user explicitly saves an edit. Grocery aggregation uses saved ingredients. Manual meals without ingredient snapshots cannot yield a complete list. Groceries are grouped by category, unknown IDs are retained under Other, and different saved preparation states remain separate.

Purchase checkboxes are saved per account after migration 0005. Only the most recently edited list is retained; its signature includes scope, people, saved ingredient states, amounts and costs. A changed list starts unmarked and replaces the previous state on the next edit. Concurrent edits in separate tabs are last-write-wins.

The four-slot generated planner remains unchanged. Optional pre-/post-workout meal slots can be manually created and swapped to compatible tagged recipes; they are not automatically scheduled or a claim of a required timing window. Manually entered meals may be unpriced or exceed a budget, with existing warnings. Energy-comparison filtering is not a weight-goal prescription, and no goal-based nutrient guarantees are made.

## Estimates and imagery
Nutrition and INR costs are provisional ingredient-based estimates, not verified product labels, current local quotes or medical advice. Added ingredient records specify a preparation state, but the generic FoodData Central homepage links are starting points, not validation of every assigned value. Fiber is unavailable. Unquantified water, herbs and seasonings are excluded. Actual brands, portion yield, method, prices and cross-contact can change results. Three added recipes have dish-specific generated illustrations; the other recipes use existing imagery where available or a generic generated illustration. The library hero is illustrative, not a photo of each recipe.

## Rollback
Revert the recipe-related source, component, style, image and test changes together. Preserve existing snapshots and unrelated work. Grocery purchase persistence depends on migration 0005; do not drop its table without an approved backup and data-retention decision. New catalog IDs referenced in plans created after this change may lose catalog-based details if the expansion is removed, although their stored snapshots should remain intact. Test rollback against an isolated database before applying it elsewhere.
