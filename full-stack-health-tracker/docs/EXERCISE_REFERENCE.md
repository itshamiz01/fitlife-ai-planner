# Read-only exercise reference

The exercise reference presents 36 general movement descriptions for educational use. It is not the unfinished personalized workout generator, a prescription, an assessment of ability, or medical clearance. It does not generate or change workouts or meals, record workout history, recommend sets/repetitions/loads, or use AI. Descriptions have **not** been reviewed by a qualified exercise professional; the UI says so and asks users to seek individual advice before trying a movement.

Before displaying the descriptions, the component asks about urgent symptoms, restrictions, injury or mobility limitations, and pregnancy/postpartum. Urgent symptoms receive urgent-help messaging. Missing, unsure or positive answers, age under 18, and age 65+ do not receive automatic guidance and are directed to a professional. These are conservative software gates, not a validated clinical screening tool or a statement that the person cannot exercise. Answers are held in browser component state and are not persisted or sent to a server or AI. Existing profile age is read; no other profile field is changed. Stop for pain, dizziness or discomfort.

Each card contains a name, purpose, muscles, equipment, draft steps, breathing, common mistakes, precautions and possible easier/harder alternatives. Descriptions are searchable by movement, muscle or equipment. A reviewed video is explicitly unavailable for every card. No YouTube videos or other external media are embedded or linked in the release because the development candidates have not been watched and independently reviewed for the correct movement, equipment, accessibility and safety. They may contain conflicting instructions or other exercises; search-result titles and snippets are not verification. The unreviewed candidate manifest stays in the separate local development draft, not this release. Videos may be added after individual review and suitable link/availability checks. This is not a claim that the full workout system or personalized hydration is complete.

No migration or database write is introduced. To roll back, remove the read-only component and its `views.tsx` rendering plus its CSS; this does not remove any profile, meal, workout, water or other records. `scripts/verify-exercise-reference-browser.cjs` uses a synthetic isolated database and checks safety gates, no writes, 36 cards, search, truthful media fallback, no YouTube requests, and viewport widths of 320/390/768/1440. It does not demonstrate clinical correctness, independent professional review, real-device accessibility, or exercise safety. Broader exercise-professional review remains outstanding.
